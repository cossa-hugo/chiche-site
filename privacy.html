Your data

English translation of Tes données. The French version is the reference.

What the app stores

Your e-mail address and your password (stored hashed, in unreadable form), your nickname, your leagues, your contracts, your stakes and your points. If you report a player, the text of your report. If the app runs into an error, its technical message, with nothing you typed, deleted after 30 days.

Your sports activities

Only if you link your watch: the date, type, distance and duration of your activities from the last 90 days, provided by Garmin. They are used only to compute your odds and to settle your contracts. To settle a contract, only elapsed time and distance are extracted from the file of the activity concerned, never your location or your heart rate; that extract is deleted after 8 days. This data is stored with Supabase and nowhere else. It is neither sold nor shared with any third party, and no artificial intelligence system processes it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unlinking your watch from the “Carnet” tab: your activities are then deleted.

Usage measurement

To understand what players enjoy, Chiche records the days you open the app and five actions: sharing an invitation, a contract or a verdict, opening a dare, viewing the weekly recap. Nothing else: not what you type, not your location, nothing that comes from Garmin. These records stay with Supabase, are read only by the publisher, and are deleted with your account and after 13 months. You can opt out from the “Carnet” tab: what was recorded is then deleted.

What other players see

The members of your leagues see your nickname, your contracts, your stakes and your points. They see neither your e-mail address nor your activities.

What the app does not do

No advertising, no selling of data, no tracking of your location. Points have no monetary value.

Where the data is kept

With Supabase, in an Amazon Web Services data centre located in Frankfurt, in the European Union. The code used to change a forgotten password is e-mailed by Resend, a US company, from its servers in Ireland: for that purpose it receives your e-mail address and that message, nothing else.

Deleting your account

From the “Carnet” tab, at any time. Your e-mail address, your password, your nickname and your activities are erased. Your contracts in play count as forfeits. Your past bets remain under an anonymous name, otherwise the other players' accounts would no longer add up.

Your rights

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can ask to access it, correct it, erase it or receive a copy of it by writing to the address below. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country: the CNIL in France, the CNPD in Luxembourg.

Who is responsible

This site sets no cookies and does not measure its audience.

The waiting list: if you leave your e-mail on the sign-up page, it is used only to tell you when the app is released, is shared with no one, and is deleted at the latest one year later. To be removed, write to the address above.
